Usage rules

Use Line only between grouped items — a form-section boundary, a menu-item separator, a list-row divider. Never as decoration.

Prefer space/24 or space/32 before/after the rule. Don't chain multiple lines back-to-back — that's a spacing problem, not a divider problem.

Do
  • Use one Divider between two related-but-separable groups
  • Bind the fill to border/default
  • Give the rule 24–32px breathing space on both sides
Don't
  • Use two dividers in a row
  • Hardcode #D8D6D0
  • Use Line as a decorative graphic element
